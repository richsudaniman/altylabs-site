RJ Industrial Supply Inc.Industrial supply distributor, North Bay, Ontario

How RJ Industrial Supply cut PO entry from 8 minutes to about a minute

About fifty purchase orders a day were being typed in by hand. An AI order-intake system now reads them, translates shorthand to exact SKUs and drafts the sales order in NetSuite for one-click approval.

28 hsaved every week (~104 h a month)
85% fasterper order
5 weeksto deploy on NetSuite

The bottleneck

  • About 6.7 hours a day lost to typing. At ~8 minutes per PO across about 50 orders a day, manual order entry consumed about 33.5 hours a week.

What we built

Deployed in about 5 weeks on top of their existing NetSuite setup, with full client ownership and zero per-order fees.

  • Multi-format intake. Reads emailed PDFs and phone photos of handwritten orders automatically.
  • Shorthand translation. Maps trade shorthand, such as “3/4 copper 90s x 20”, directly to exact SKUs in their item catalog.
  • One-click NetSuite drafting. Builds the complete sales order inside NetSuite for human review and one-click approval.
