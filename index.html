Questions

Before you book.

We tried AI tools before and they didn't stick. Why would this be different?

Most tools fail at adoption. A general tool sits beside the work, so using it is extra effort. We build the system into the work itself, on the software your team already uses, and stay through the first weeks until it is the normal way of doing the job.

We don't have a tech team. Can we actually maintain this?

Yes. It runs on the tools you already use, comes with plain-language documentation, and each role is trained on its own tasks. We stay on after launch to fix and tune it.

How much does this cost?

It depends on the scope. Every stage is scoped and quoted before it starts, so you know the price before any work begins. The ROI calculator shows what the time you get back is worth.

What if we're not sure automation will actually help?

That is what the readiness assessment is for. We map where the hours go and give you a plain answer: fix, buy or build. Sometimes the answer is that you don't need us yet. You can get a first read now with the readiness check.

Do we need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Each stage is scoped and quoted on its own, so you commit to one stage at a time.

How long does a typical implementation take?

Usually 3 to 6 weeks. A full custom app takes longer. You get a written scope and timeline before any build starts.

Stop losing skilled hours to repeat work.

Show us one piece of work that ties up your team. We will tell you whether a system fits, and what the first step should be.
